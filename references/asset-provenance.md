# Asset provenance and public-use boundary

The figure library is not presented as the author's private or proprietary collection. Its figure examples were collected for study from publicly accessible academic literature and independently reimplemented as executable Python assets.

- Original publications, figures, labels, and underlying research results remain attributable to their respective authors and publishers.
- The repository's Apache-2.0 license applies to original code and documentation contributed to Econfigure Skill. It does not relicense third-party publications, institutional templates, or other material for which the project does not hold rights.
- Runtime previews must be generated from the included scripts. Do not add screenshots or copied original figures to the distributable skill.
- New assets must record a public-source note in the private development workspace, pass a redistribution review, and expose only the runtime semantic contract in the public manifest.
- Fixture data should be synthetic or sufficiently transformed for demonstration. Do not imply that demonstration values reproduce a published empirical result.
